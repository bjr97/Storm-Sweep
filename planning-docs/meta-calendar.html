Off-Season Positioning (June–August)
☀️
Mid-Summer Strategy: Tornado season peaks April–May. June–August is the off-season — fewer people are thinking about shelters. Your job is to make them think about it anyway. Lead with "get it done before next season" framing + the free kit incentive. This is your brand-building window. Don't chase urgency — build trust instead.

Content Production System

How raw job footage becomes polished, scheduled social content — with minimal time from you each week.

Weekly Production Flow
1
📱
Sweeper Captures
On every job: before photo, after photo, 10-sec before video clip, 10-sec after video clip. Uploaded to app before leaving. Takes 3 minutes.
2
🤖
AI Production
Raw photos → branded before/after graphic via Canva template or AI tool (Adobe Firefly, CapCut). Caption drafted using Claude with job notes as input.
3
✅
Your Weekly Review
Every Sunday or Monday — review the week's scheduled posts in Buffer. Approve, edit, or swap. Takes 15–20 minutes max. Paid ads reviewed separately before launch.
4
📅
Auto-Schedule
Buffer publishes to FB + IG automatically at optimal times. You get a weekly performance summary. Boost top posts via Meta Ads Manager manually.
⏱️
Time commitment: Once the system is running, your weekly time investment is roughly 20 min Sunday review + 15 min ad check bi-weekly. Sweepers handle raw capture. AI handles production. Buffer handles scheduling. You stay in control without running it daily.

Sweeper Photo Brief
What Sweepers Capture on Every Job
Shared with all Sweepers · Required
Photo 1 — Before Wide
Stand in doorway or on steps looking down into the shelter. Capture the full interior. Natural light or phone flashlight. Do NOT clean anything first.
Photo 2 — Before Detail
One close-up of the worst area — mold, debris, rust, whatever tells the story. This is the "gross" shot that drives engagement.
Video — Before Clip
10-second slow pan around the interior before work begins. Phone steady, no talking needed. Portrait/vertical orientation.
Photo 3 — After Wide
Same angle as Photo 1. Clean, lit if LED installed, supplies visible if stocked. Make it look great.
Video — After Clip
10-second pan matching before clip angle. Slow, steady. Let the clean space speak for itself.
Customer Permission
Ask at wrap-up: "Mind if we share before/after photos on our social media? No personal info, just the shelter." Most say yes. Log yes/no in app.

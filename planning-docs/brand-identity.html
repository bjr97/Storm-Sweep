04 — Services
What We Do
🧹
Deep Clean & Deodorize
Full scrub-down of walls, floors, and ceiling. Mold treatment, odor elimination, and debris removal. The foundation of every visit.
💡
Lighting Upgrades
LED panel install, battery backup lighting, and motion-triggered emergency lights. No more stumbling in the dark.
🎒
Supply Stocking
Custom emergency kits — water, first aid, weather radio, comfort items. Refreshed annually for members.
🔩
Structural & Door Upgrades
Hatch door inspection and replacement, hinge reinforcement, locking mechanism upgrades, and interior handle installs.

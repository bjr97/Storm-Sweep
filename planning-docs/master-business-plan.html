Partner Referral Tracking (Tech)
How to Track Partner Referrals in the App
Add a referral_source field to the bookings table in Supabase. At booking, the customer selects how they heard about Storm Sweep — include partner names as options (e.g. "Referred by ABC Roofing"). Each partner gets a unique referral code embedded in their leave-behind QR code → auto-populated at booking. Admin dash shows referrals per partner monthly → triggers payout. Partners can be tracked in a simple partners table with name, type, referral_code, total_referrals, total_payout.

Trash & Junk Removal Policy
🗑️
Junk & Trash Policy — Clear Scope of Work
Storm Sweep is a cleaning and upgrade service, not a junk removal service. The following policy applies to all jobs:

  • Normal debris & pest waste: Included in every clean. Swept, vacuumed, bagged, and left for customer at exterior.
  • Old small items (expired supplies, boxes, etc.): Set aside neatly for customer to review. Do not discard without explicit customer permission.
  • Excessive junk or furniture: Not included. If customer's pre-booking photo shows excessive items, admin will contact customer to clear before scheduling. Sweeper is NOT responsible for heavy item removal.
  • On-site junk discovery: If sweeper arrives and finds excessive items beyond what was in the pre-booking photo, they must contact admin before proceeding. A junk surcharge ($35–$75) may be applied with customer approval, or job may be rescheduled.
  • Hazardous materials: Immediately stop work, photograph, contact admin. Do not remove.

Tax Transparency Policy for ICs
📋
1099 Contractor Tax Policy
Storm Sweep hires sweepers as independent contractors (1099-NEC). The following is communicated clearly at onboarding:

  • Storm Sweep does NOT withhold federal or state income taxes from IC pay.
  • Sweepers earning $600+ in a calendar year will receive a 1099-NEC by January 31.
  • Sweepers are responsible for tracking their own income and paying estimated quarterly taxes to the IRS (Form 1040-ES).
  • Self-employment tax (15.3%) applies to net IC income. Sweepers should set aside ~25–30% of earnings for taxes.
  • Business expenses (equipment, supplies, mileage) are likely deductible — sweepers should consult a tax professional.
  • Storm Sweep will provide an earnings summary dashboard in the sweeper app for easy tax-year reporting.

This policy is acknowledged via e-signature during IC onboarding.

AI Photo Pre-Booking Tool
🤖 How It Works
At booking, customer uploads 1–2 photos of their shelter interior before confirming. The AI tool (Claude Vision via API) grades the photo on a scale:

Grade A–B: Normal condition. Auto-approved. Booking proceeds.
Grade C: Moderate junk or debris. Admin notified for manual review within 2 hours. Customer shown: "Your shelter has some extra items — we'll confirm your appointment shortly."
Grade D–F: Excessive junk, large items, or potential hazard. Booking paused. Admin contacts customer: junk must be cleared OR junk surcharge ($35–$75) is offered before confirmation.

What AI looks for: Large furniture/appliances, bags of trash, water accumulation, visible mold walls (schedule structural review), rodent evidence (flag to admin).

Manual final pass: All C grades reviewed by admin before approval. D/F grades always get a personal call. This keeps the AI as a filter, not a gatekeeper.

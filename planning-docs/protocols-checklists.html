CH · 05
Customer Scripts
Word-for-word guidance for common on-site situations. Adapt naturally — don't read robotically.
Arrival & Greeting
Standard Arrival
Sweeper
"Hi [Name]! I'm [Your Name] from Storm Sweep — great to meet you. I'm here for your shelter service today. Mind if I take a quick look at the shelter before we get started?"
Always use the customer's first name. You have it in the app. It matters.
Presenting Upgrade Recommendations
LED Lighting Upsell
Sweeper
"Everything's cleaned up and looking great. One thing I noticed — there's no lighting down here right now. In a real tornado situation, you'd be going in blind. We offer a battery-powered LED install that takes about 20 minutes. It's $89 and I can do it right now while I'm here. Want me to show you what it looks like?"
Customer
"How long does the battery last?"
Sweeper
"The main panel runs about 8 hours on a charge — plenty for any storm situation. The emergency backup light is motion-triggered and lasts even longer. Most people forget it's even down there until they need it."
Don't push if they decline. Log it in the app and move on warmly. Upsell pressure kills reviews.
Supply Kit Upsell
Sweeper
"One more thing — the shelter's clean, but I didn't see any emergency supplies down here. Water, first aid, that kind of thing. We put together a basic kit for $49 — water, a first aid kit, a hand-crank weather radio, and a couple of other things. I can leave it in the shelter today if you want."
Frame it around preparedness, not fear. Oklahoma families know the risk — you're just solving a real gap for them.
Storm Ready Membership Pitch (Non-Members)
Sweeper
"Before I go — are you on our Storm Ready plan? It's $249 a year and covers two cleanings, a supply kit refresh, and bumps you to priority scheduling before tornado season. Basically pays for itself on the second visit. A lot of our customers switch after their first single-sweep."
Customer
"That sounds good, how do I sign up?"
Sweeper
"I can send you the link right now — you sign up in the app and it takes about 2 minutes. Today's visit would count toward your first Storm Ready service."
Handling Objections
Customer: "That seems expensive."
Sweeper
"Totally fair. For a one-time clean, $149 is the flat rate — no surprises. We keep it straightforward. If you want to get more value out of it, the Storm Ready plan is $249 for the whole year and covers two visits. That's $124 per cleaning. Most people feel better about that once they do the math."
Customer: "I need to think about the upgrade."
Sweeper
"No problem at all — I'll log the recommendation in your service report and you'll see it in your portal. If you want to add it later, just book through the app and we'll come out. No pressure."
Always end positively. The report will remind them — you don't have to.
Wrap-Up & Goodbye
Standard Sign-Off
Sweeper
"All done! Your before and after photos are already in your portal — you should get a text with the link in just a minute. If you think of anything or have questions, you can reply to that text or reach us through the app. Really appreciate you choosing Storm Sweep — stay safe out there."
CH · 06
Policies & Standards
Non-negotiable rules that protect the customer, the brand, and you.
📸
Photo Policy
Before and after photos are required on every job — no exceptions. Jobs without photos will not be paid out until photos are submitted. Photos go to the customer portal automatically.
🕐
Punctuality
Arrive within your booked time window. If you will be more than 15 minutes late, notify admin immediately via the app. Customers receive automated reminders — don't be a no-show.
💬
Customer Communication
All customer communication happens through the Storm Sweep app or the automated text system. Do not give customers your personal cell number or solicit off-platform bookings. Violation results in IC contract termination.
💧
Water & Hazard Protocol
If you find standing water, structural damage, mold beyond surface level, or pest infestation, stop work immediately. Photograph the issue and notify admin. Do not attempt structural repairs yourself.
🚭
On-Property Conduct
No smoking, vaping, or alcohol on any job site. Treat every customer's home with respect — don't enter other areas of the property without permission. Keep noise minimal.
⭐
Review Standard
Storm Sweep aims for 4.8+ on Google at all times. The app sends the review request automatically. Never ask customers to leave a specific rating — just ask them to share their honest experience.
🔒
Privacy & Property
Customer photos, addresses, and contact info are confidential. Never share or post customer information or property photos on personal social media. Before/after content for Storm Sweep's social must be approved by admin.
📋
Checklist Completion
All checklist items must be marked in the app before completing a job. Skipped items require a written note explaining why. Habitual incomplete checklists will be reviewed and can affect IC standing.
🚨
Zero Tolerance: Theft, misrepresentation, off-platform solicitation, or falsifying job records results in immediate IC contract termination and forfeiture of pending earnings. Storm Sweep takes its reputation seriously.
